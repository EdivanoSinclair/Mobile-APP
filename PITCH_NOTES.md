# 2-minute investor pitch — KasiConnect

Good morning, investors. Imagine looking for a job, a plumber, or a small local business, but the information is scattered across different WhatsApp groups, social media pages and word of mouth. That makes useful opportunities easy to miss.

KasiConnect is a mobile-friendly community platform that brings local jobs, services and small businesses into one easy-to-search place. People can browse opportunities by category and area, search for what they need, save listings for later, and share a new opportunity with their community.

Our first users are job seekers, residents, small business owners and local service providers in communities around Johannesburg. For residents, KasiConnect makes opportunities easier to discover. For small businesses, it offers a simple way to reach nearby customers. For community organisations, it could become a channel for sharing trusted opportunities.

Unlike using several separate social media groups, KasiConnect is designed around local discovery: one directory, clear categories and area filters. We plan to start with a small pilot, partner with community groups and business associations, and build trust through listing moderation and verification.

Our potential business model includes optional promoted listings for businesses, with core community access remaining free. Before launch, we will validate demand and test pricing with real users.

The prototype demonstrates the core experience. Our next step is to add secure accounts, a shared database, verified listings and reporting tools.

KasiConnect helps opportunity travel a little closer to home. Thank you.
